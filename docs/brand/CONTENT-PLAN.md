## Rules for every post
- **Facts only.** Every number comes from the site or the chain. No returns, users or deposits until they exist.
- **One idea per post,** with a picture from the real product (screenshot of the site, chip, log). No stock art.
- **Plain words.** Say what it does, not what it "unlocks".
- **Tag @XLayerOfficial / @TapeOutWorld only when the post is about their tech** (launch, REF, chips, submission). Not on every post.
- **Fund posts end with:** "Rules, not advice. Read them before you invest."
- **Time:** 3 pm Lagos (9 am New York, 10 pm Singapore). Start the composer 15 minutes early.

## Daily routine (15 minutes)

2. Reply to 3–5 posts from @TapeOutWorld, @XLayerOfficial, xStocks and other X Layer builders. Add something real (a screenshot, a number), never "great project".
3. Reply to anyone who comments on our posts the same day.
